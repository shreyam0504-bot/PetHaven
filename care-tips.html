Pet care tips

Short, simple guidelines to help you care for your new family member.

For Dogs

Daily walks, fresh water, regular vet check-ups and positive training. Give them playtime and a safe place to rest.

For Cats

Provide clean litter, scratching posts, hiding spots and gentle play. Schedule yearly health check-ups.

For Rabbits

Offer hay, fresh greens and safe space to hop. Avoid loud noises and handle them gently and calmly.

For Birds

Clean cage, perches, toys and daily out-of-cage time if possible. Keep them away from smoke and strong fumes.

First-time Pet Parents

Start small: set a routine, learn basic body language, and ask vets or trainers when in doubt. Patience matters more than perfection.
